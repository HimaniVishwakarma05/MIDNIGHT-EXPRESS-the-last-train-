MIDNIGHT EXPRESS

THE LAST TRAIN

At midnight a lone steam locomotive runs a ghostly line through dark forests and abandoned stations. Collect tickets, coins and fuel. Escape the ghost trains. Survive as long as you can.

HOW TO PLAY

  • Controls: ← / A and → / D switch lanes · Space brakes · P or Esc pauses · Enter starts
  • Collect: ticket +10 · coin +5 · fuel can +15 and +20% fuel · green token +20 and +35% brake energy
  • Avoid: broken track, rocks and ghost trains. Each hit costs one life.
  • Emergency brake: slows the world to 35% for 2 seconds and costs 35% brake energy. A short cooldown follows.
  • Interact: swipe or tap the left and right sides to change lanes · tap the middle or press W to whistle
  • Fuel and lives: fuel burns over time and an empty tank ends the run. You start with 3 lives.
